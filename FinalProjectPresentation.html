Final Project Presentation
Monday, December 2, 2019



Your final project presentation will be a demonstration of your software solution for the course project.  At this time, you will produce deliverables for the presentation that will communicate your approach, solution, and demonstrate a working solution that implements the functional and technical requirements for the course project.  You may frame your discussion with a PowerPoint presentation.  Specific topics to cover are:

Description of your development methodology & how well (or not) it worked
Technical Architecture and System Design
Messaging Desgin and Solution Architecture
Pain Points (technical and organizational)

You should expect to present your final project in detail, demonstrating every aspect of your solution, messaging, services, logging, and data storage solutions.
